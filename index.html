3D Camera & Controls Reference

• WASD / Arrow Keys: Pan 3D camera across terrain
• Right Click + Drag: Orbit / Rotate 3D camera view
• Mouse Wheel: Smooth 3D zoom in/out
• Left Click / Drag: Place building or conveyor line
• R Key: Rotate building direction (North/East/South/West)
• Q Key: Pipet tool (sample building under cursor)
• E Key: Open Research Technology Tree
• Spacebar: Pause / Resume simulation time
• Keys 1 to 9: Quick-select hotbar slot
